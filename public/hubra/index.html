What it does

Everything a club board does, in one place.

The board works in the same app as the members. Everyone sees what they need, and nothing more.

Members

Applications, membership tiers, offices and a member directory each member opts into.

Finance

Fee schedules, Swiss QR bills, automatic reminders and payment matching from a camt.054 bank file.

Events and training

Recurring sessions, sign-ups, guests and attendance with one tap, plus push reminders.

Polls and votes

Vote online, by proxy too, for all members or just one group.

Security

Passkeys, hosting in Europe, a key per club and encrypted backups.

Plugins

Payments, bookings, inventory, documents, keys, a till, a Discord bridge and more, from the plugin catalogue.

$ show security

Built for members' data.

Clubs keep addresses, payments and sometimes data about children.

Hosted in Europe

Servers and backups in European data centres, following the Swiss FADP and the EU GDPR.

A key per club

Each club has its own space and its own keys for confidential data such as door codes.

Encrypted backups

Daily, and encrypted before they leave the server, so not even the storage provider can read them.

Sign-in

Passkeys, one-time codes and a mail for every sign-in from a new device. Clubs can require 2FA for the board.

Audit log

The board sees who changed what and when. Support access needs a reason, is time-limited and shows as a banner.

Run it yourself

With Docker Compose or Kubernetes, for federations or clubs with their own IT.

What's next

Clubs suggest and vote on ideas on support.hubra.club. The full list is on the roadmap.

  • nextfinanceTWINT for invoices and donations, booked automatically, plus a donation page per club.
  • nextfinanceSimple annual accounts: chart of accounts, year close, and a balance sheet as a PDF for the AGM.
  • nextclubDocument library for statutes, minutes and forms, visible by role or group.
  • laterrolesRoles per group, like "manage the youth team's events".
  • laterplatformYour own domain, such as members.yourclub.ch.
  • doneeventsRecurring trainings and attendance.
  • donesecurityPasskeys and two-factor sign-in.
  • donefinanceRecurring fees and a monthly statement.
  • donepluginsDiscord bot for clubs.

Background

Why I built it

I wanted club software that looks good, has every feature I wanted, and is priced so that even small clubs can afford it.

It started for VollBuild, the makerspace club I'm part of, and grew from there into what it is today. Hubra isn't public or in production yet, and no other clubs are on it so far.

Hubra is closed source. The code lives on GitHub for the automation and infrastructure as code that comes with it.
