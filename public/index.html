Labdex

U2 · tool · homelab inventory

A small inventory for homelabs that treats zones and names as first-class. Describe the lab once and Labdex generates DNS, firewall aliases and an Ansible inventory. One container, SQLite, under 100 MB of RAM.

zonesTrust zones and naming rules, linted
opnsenseFirewall aliases, synced
dnsA, AAAA, CNAME and SRV records
ansibleInventory, plan first, then apply
