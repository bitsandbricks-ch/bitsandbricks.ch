Hubra

hubra.club · club management

Club software for Swiss clubs. Members, fees with Swiss QR bills, events and training sessions, votes and groups in one app. It's hosted in Europe, has no tracking, and works in German and English, in light and dark.

Labdex

homelab inventory · IPAM · automation

A small inventory for homelabs that treats zones and names as first-class. Describe networks, hosts and services once, and Labdex generates what the rest of the lab needs. It sits between a spreadsheet and NetBox: one container, SQLite, under 100 MB of RAM.

OPNsenseDNSAnsibleProxmoxPython
About Labdex Docs
Zones & namingEvery host gets a zone and a functional name, checked against your rules.
Firewall aliasesGenerated per network, zone and host, synced to OPNsense.
DNS & AnsibleA/AAAA, CNAME and SRV records, plus a ready Ansible inventory.
Plan, then applyChanges to other systems are shown first and applied after review.
