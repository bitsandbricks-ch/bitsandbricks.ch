Labdex

U2 · tool · homelab inventory

A small inventory for homelabs, because NetBox is steep and heavy for a lab. Describe the lab once and Labdex generates DNS, firewall aliases and an Ansible inventory. One container, SQLite, under 100 MB of RAM. I use it in my own lab.
