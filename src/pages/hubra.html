Hubra

Club software for Swiss clubs. Members, fees, events, votes and groups in one app, run for the club with updates and encrypted backups. All a member needs is a browser.

What it does

Everything a club board does, in one place.

The board works in the same app as the members. Everyone sees what they need, and nothing more.

Members

Applications, membership tiers, offices and a member directory each member opts into.

Finance

Fee schedules, Swiss QR bills, automatic reminders and payment matching from a camt.054 bank file.

Events and training

Recurring sessions, sign-ups, guests and attendance with one tap, plus push reminders.

Polls and votes

Vote online, by proxy too, for all members or just one group.

Security

Passkeys, hosting in Europe, a key per club and encrypted backups.

Plugins

Payments, bookings, inventory, documents, keys, a till, a Discord bridge and more, from the plugin catalogue.
