IS-IS uses levels instead of areas: level 1 inside an area, level 2 between areas. It runs
directly on layer 2 rather than over IP. Many terms map to OSPF ones, see
[OSPF areas](/kb/routing/igp/ospf/ospf-areas/) and [LSA types](/kb/routing/igp/ospf/lsa-types/).
