---
title: "BFD in two minutes"
description: "Fast failure detection that routing protocols can subscribe to."
tags: [bfd]
verified: "2026-04"
sample: true
---

BFD sends small hellos, often every 50 to 300 ms, and tells OSPF, IS-IS or BGP when a
neighbour stops answering. See [OSPF timers](/kb/routing/igp/ospf/ospf-timers/).
